Active vs Passive Sensor Explorer

Compare active and passive Earth-observation sensors with animated visuals showing how each type measures the planet.

Passive optical sensor

Passive sensors measure sunlight reflected or emitted by the Earth. They need daylight (for reflected light) and cannot see through clouds.

Energy source: Sun Works at night: No Sees through cloud: No Examples: Landsat, Sentinel-2, MODIS

Diagrams are schematic and animated for illustration, not to scale. Real sensors vary widely in wavelength, resolution and orbit.
