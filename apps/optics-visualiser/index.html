Formulas

N = F ÷ D  ·  M = F ÷ fe  ·  exit pupil = D ÷ M

true FOV ≈ apparent FOV ÷ M  ·  Dawes limit ≈ 116″ ÷ D(mm)

light grasp ≈ (D ÷ 7 mm)²

True field of view uses the simple approximation; it is less accurate for wide-field eyepieces. Dawes’ limit and the magnification rules of thumb are guides, not guarantees: seeing, optical quality and your eyes matter.
