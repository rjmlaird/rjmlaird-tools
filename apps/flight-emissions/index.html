          Method, constants and limits

          Emissions = great-circle distance × routing uplift × haul-band factor × cabin multiplier × passengers (× 2 for a return), then × the non-CO₂ multiplier if enabled, then reduced by the SAF share × its lifecycle saving. This is a screening estimate; real emissions depend on aircraft, load factor, routing and weather.

          The factors were carried over from an earlier prototype that attributed them to DEFRA/DESNZ and ICAO 2024 tables. They have not been checked against the published values here, so verify them before using results for reporting. Distances use the haversine formula on a 6,371 km sphere. The built-in list has 23 major airports; use custom coordinates for others.
