Spiral galaxy

Spiral galaxies have a flattened disc with spiral arms, ongoing star formation and a central bulge. The Milky Way and Andromeda are examples.

Star formation: High Typical mass: 10^10–10^12 solar masses Examples: Milky Way, Andromeda, Whirlpool

Diagrams are schematic, not to scale. Real galaxies vary widely in size, colour and structure.
