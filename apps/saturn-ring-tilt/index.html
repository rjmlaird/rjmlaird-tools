Saturn Ring Tilt Visualiser

See how Saturn’s rings open and close as seen from Earth, computed from a Saturn-centric ephemeris model rather than a fixed sinusoidal curve.

Ring tilt (B): – View: –

Tilt is computed from simplified planetary orbital elements and Saturn’s north-pole direction (a low-precision ephemeris, typically good to a few hundredths of a degree). Ring-plane crossings and north/south geometry follow from the sign of B. For survey-grade work, use JPL Horizons.
