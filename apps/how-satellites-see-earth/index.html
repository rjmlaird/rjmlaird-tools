Earth Observation Explainer

How satellites see Earth

Satellites do not simply take photographs. They measure reflected and emitted energy in selected wavelength bands, then combine those measurements to reveal vegetation, water, clouds, fire, land use and climate-related change.

MVP data notice: this demonstration uses a synthetic landscape and simulated spectral responses. It is designed to explain the principles behind satellite imagery; it does not display real Copernicus Sentinel data. A later version can use Copernicus Browser or Sentinel Hub data layers.
