Escape Velocity Calculator

Calculate the escape velocity for planets and moons, with a simple illustration of each world.

Escape velocity: –
Formula: v = √(2GM/r)

Escape velocity is the minimum speed needed to break free from a body’s gravity without further propulsion, ignoring atmosphere and other bodies. Selecting a planet fills in its mean mass and radius; choosing “Custom” lets you enter your own values.
