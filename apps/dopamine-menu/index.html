Dopamine Menu

A quick way to pick a short, enjoyable or restorative break from your own menu. “Dopamine menu” is a popular metaphor; this app doesn’t measure or change anything in your body or brain.

      Privacy, data and limitations

      This is a personal activity chooser, not a diagnostic or treatment tool. Your menu is saved in this browser unless you export it. You can clear your saved data at any time.

      This page is an informal usability experiment: does a ready-made menu make it easier to choose a break than an open question? It is not clinical research. It doesn’t score breaks, track streaks, or treat returning to work as the goal.


      Off by default. If you turn it on, the date, the title of each activity you choose, and any feedback answer are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. It is included when you export, and removed when you clear your data.
