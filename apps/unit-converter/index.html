Unit converter

Convert a value between common units of length, mass, temperature, volume, area, time and speed.

Common conversions

    How it works

    Each value is converted through a base unit for its category (for example metres for length, kelvin for temperature). Results shown on screen are rounded to at most 8 significant digits, but the calculation itself is not rounded first.

    US and Imperial gallons are different sizes: 1 US gallon is about 3.785 litres, while 1 Imperial gallon is about 4.546 litres. Temperature conversions are for temperature readings, not for differences between two temperatures.
